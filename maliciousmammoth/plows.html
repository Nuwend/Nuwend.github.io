Plows

We make exactly four plows, and each one is named for a member of the herd. A small lineup means that every model gets our full attention, and you never have to wade through a catalogue to find the one that fits your vehicle. Here’s the whole herd at a glance.

All four share the same curved tusk blade, thick steel, and safety orange paint. What changes is the size, so choose by what you drive.

The Calf

Our smallest plow, named for the youngest member of the herd. It’s light enough to mount in a few minutes and wide enough to clear a path to the mailbox, the shed, or the back door. If your plowing vehicle has handlebars or a lawn mower deck, this one is yours.

The Tusker

The everyday plow, and the one most people take home. It fits most pickup trucks, clears a standard driveway in a few passes, and has the tusk shaped blade that rolls snow aside instead of piling it in front of you. When people say Malicious Mammoth, they mean this one.

The Bull

Built for work trucks and commercial lots, the Bull has thicker steel and a reinforced cutting edge for heavy, wet snow and the occasional curb. If you plow for a living, or for a whole neighborhood, this plow is made for long shifts.

The Matriarch

She leads the herd. The Matriarch mounts to loaders and big machines, and her wide blade can clear a warehouse lot or an airport apron in a single pass. When the storm is the kind people name, this is the plow you send out.
