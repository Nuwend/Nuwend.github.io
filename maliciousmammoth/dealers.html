Dealers

You can’t buy a Malicious Mammoth plow from a website, and we like it that way. Every plow is sold and fitted by a dealer who knows your local snow, so it leaves the lot properly mounted on your vehicle. Below you’ll find where to meet the herd and when to order it.

Where to Find Us

Our dealers are in the places where winter takes itself seriously. Each one carries the full herd, and the staff can measure your vehicle and fit the right plow while you wait.

When to Order

Snow doesn’t send a warning, so we run on a calendar. Order early and your plow will be on your vehicle before the first flake falls. Order late, and you’ll be plowing with a shovel.

The Malicious Mammoth ordering season
When What Happens
August The order window opens, and dealers start taking reservations.
September Fitting season. Bring your vehicle in to be measured for a mount.
October The first plows ship, in time for the first frost.
December 1 The last day to order and be sure of delivery before the first big storm.
March and April Off season. Dealers sharpen and service plows, and accept trade-ins.
